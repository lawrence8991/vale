Why now

The rules keep tightening. Your budget doesn’t.

Letting a commercial building below EPC E is now unlawful. ESOS demands action plans, not paperwork. SECR puts your energy use in the annual report, where your board and your auditors read it. And the proposed 2030 standards will raise the bar again.

Most facilities teams are expected to manage all of this on top of the day job — with energy prices that punish every wasted kilowatt and buildings that were never metered properly in the first place.

That is the job we take off your desk. One partner who audits the estate, keeps every certificate current, watches the meters, and only ever recommends work that pays for itself.

MEES — since April 2023

Commercial property below EPC E cannot lawfully be let, even under existing leases.

ESOS — Phase 3

Large undertakings must audit, publish an action plan, and report progress against it.

SECR — every year

Energy and carbon figures go in the annual accounts. Estimates don’t survive scrutiny.

On the horizon

Government has consulted on raising the minimum to EPC B by 2030. The earlier you move, the cheaper it is.
